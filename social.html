Some Social Platforms To Find Horror Content




Explore haunted communities, chilling YouTube channels, and eerie podcasts where the unknown comes alive. These platforms are where believers gather, investigators share evidence, and the curious get lost in the dark.




🕸️ YouTube Channels — Visual Encounters

1. Nukes Top 5
https://www.youtube.com/@NukesTop5

Description: Top 5 lists of scary things! Scary videos of ghosts caught on camera! Creepy, strange, and unusual paranormal events! Unexplained videos, the supernatural, monsters and mysterious creatures, and all sorts of scary things caught on camera. All the stuff that will keep you up at night.

2. Twin Paranormal
https://www.youtube.com/@TwinParanormal

Description: SCI-FI/PARANORMAL
Born from twin brothers who grew up sensing something following them, this channel documents bold ghost hunts, overnight stays in haunted places, demon and skin-walker investigations, and high-adrenaline paranormal camping.

3. Sam And Colby
https://www.youtube.com/@samandcolby

Description: A high-profile paranormal duo whose content blends exploration of abandoned sites, haunted locations and audience-facing thrill. Great for users who like personality, production value, and large scale haunt hunts.

4. The Paranormal Files
https://www.youtube.com/@theparanormalfiles

Description: Every week The Paranormal Files travels around the world to ghost hunt, search for skinwalkers, investigate demons and capture paranormal evidence. Join us as we grow the spooky family, release real paranormal videos and adventure together!

5. Darkness Prevails
https://www.youtube.com/@darknessprevails

Description: Stories of the unexplained and terrifying from everyday folk all over the world. The best creepy YouTube channel for nightmare fuel and true scary stories, real ghost stories, real horror stories, real monster sightings, and more!

6. OmarGosh TV
https://www.youtube.com/@omargoshtv

Description: A creator who regularly ventures into haunted buildings, abandoned asylums and ghost-towns. He does overnight stays, records his reactions, and mixes thrill-adventure with paranormal investigation.

7. CJ Faison
https://www.youtube.com/@CJFaison

Description: This channel offers a blend of off-road adventure, ghost hunts and private investigation events (including specially curated stays at haunted properties such as the “Pocomoke Ranch”). Good for those who like both exploration and the paranormal.

8. Paranormal Quest
https://www.youtube.com/@ParanormalQuest

Description: A more serious ghost-hunting channel that travels globally to investigate reputedly haunted places. Their content leans toward longer investigations and documenting occurrences rather than quick thrills.

💬 Communities & Discussion Platforms

Reddit

1. r/Paranormal:

A global community of paranormal enthusiasts sharing personal experiences, ghost videos, unexplained phenomena, and evidence. Members discuss theories, give advice on ghost hunting, and post their own eerie encounters.

Platform: reddit.com/r/paranormal↗

2. r/Ghosts:

Focused specifically on ghost sightings and spirit activity. Users post photos, videos, and audio recordings of alleged hauntings. Great for people who want a purely ghost‑centered discussion space.

Platform: reddit.com/r/ghosts↗

3. r/UnresolvedMysteries:

Covers a broader spectrum of unsolved mysteries, including paranormal cases. A place for deep-dive discussions, theories, and community investigation.

Platform: reddit.com/UnresolvedMysteries↗

4. r/CreepyEncounters:

Dedicated to creepy experiences that may or may not be paranormal. Perfect for short horror stories and unsettling real-life encounters..

Platform: reddit.com/CreepyEncounters↗

Facebook

1. Paranormal Encounters:

A group where members worldwide share experiences, videos, and photos of paranormal activity. The community often collaborates for investigations and discussions.

Platform: Search “Paranormal Encounters” on Facebook↗

2. Ghost Hunters International:

Fans and amateur ghost hunters unite here to post findings, plan investigations, and discuss paranormal theories.

Platform: Search “Ghost Hunters International” on Facebook↗

Discord Servers – Haunted Realm, Spirit Seekers, The Unknown
These are real-time chat communities where users discuss hauntings, share stories, and sometimes even do live investigations. Discord is great for connecting directly with other enthusiasts.

Platform: Search the server names on Discord↗ or via invite links from fan communities.

X (formerly Twitter) – #ParanormalEvidence, #HauntedPlaces
Users post quick updates, photos, and videos of recent sightings or investigations. Using hashtags lets you find trending paranormal discussions globally.

Platform: twitter.com↗










📺 TV Shows & Streaming Platforms

These can also be mentioned for visitors who love long-form paranormal media.

    Ghost Adventures (Discovery+) – Zak Bagans and team explore haunted sites.
    Destination Fear (HBO Max / Discovery+) – Real investigations mixed with fear challenges.
    Kindred Spirits (Travel Channel) – More emotional, investigative paranormal show.
    The Dead Files – Psychic medium meets retired detective to solve hauntings.
    Haunted (Netflix) – Real people recount terrifying paranormal experiences.
    28 Days Haunted (Netflix) – Experimental ghost-hunting challenge based on Ed & Lorraine Warren’s theories.
    Paranormal Caught on Camera (Discovery+) – Compilation of real-life ghost footage from around the world.
